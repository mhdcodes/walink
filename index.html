What is a wa.me link?

A wa.me link is WhatsApp's official click-to-chat URL format. Anyone who clicks it opens a chat with the linked number — without saving it as a contact first. It's ideal for Instagram bios, email signatures, QR codes, business cards, and customer-support buttons.
